# Visual system

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Direction

Website and dashboard use the Terang mode from `design.md`: an editorial information board for a public masjid portal. The interface prioritizes quick scanning, transparent financial data, and clear next actions.

## Palette

- Paper: `#FBFBF7`
- Ink: `#2F4A22`
- Brand: `#79A34C`
- Accent: `#FEEF10`
- Body: `#4A5142`
- Muted: `#6B7263`
- Lines: `#E4E6DC`
- Panel: `#F0F1E8`

## Typography

Archivo and Archivo Black carry headings and action labels. IBM Plex Mono carries dates, times, labels, amounts, and metadata. Amiri remains available for Arabic accents only.

## Shape and depth

Containers use 6px corners, controls use 3px corners, and depth comes from 1px borders and flat color blocks. No gradients or drop shadows are used.

## Composition

The homepage opens with a compact white masthead, a large left-aligned identity lockup, a dark schedule rail, then yellow Friday programming, activities, finance links, and infak information. Report pages use a typographic title band, compact month controls, flat metric blocks, charts, and bordered tables.

## Interaction

Primary actions use yellow on ink. Focus rings use the brand green. Hover states shift color rather than elevation. Responsive layouts collapse grids to one or two columns below 820px and reduce navigation padding below 600px.
